1. Introduction

The Hotel Management System is a web-based application designed to automate and streamline hotel operations. Traditional manual methods for reservations, customer records, and booking administration are time-consuming and error-prone. Our platform enables customers to view available rooms, make reservations, and manage bookings online, while administrators oversee rooms, bookings, and customer information through a centralized dashboard. The result? Improved efficiency, reduced manual effort, and enhanced customer satisfaction.

2. Problem Statement

Booking Conflicts & Errors

Manual handling leads to double-bookings, inaccurate records, and frustrated guests. Overbooked rooms or missed reservations damage reputation.

Inefficient Operations

Delayed customer service, scattered record-keeping, and lack of real-time availability slow down hotel staff and increase operational costs.

Unsustainable Growth

As customer volumes grow, manual systems collapse. A digital transformation is essential for scalability and competitive advantage.

3. Objectives

General Objective

To develop a web-based Hotel Management System that facilitates room booking and hotel administration for both customers and administrators.

Customer Portal

  • Registration & secure login
  • View / search available rooms
  • Online reservations & manage bookings

Admin Capabilities

  • Manage rooms & availability
  • Maintain customer records
  • Booking oversight & analytics

Operational Gains

  • Reduce manual work by 70%
  • Intuitive interface for all users
  • Improve hotel efficiency metrics

4. Scope of the Study

In scope: Room reservations, customer registration, room browsing, booking management, admin dashboard for room & customer handling, real-time availability. The system will be a fully functional prototype for hotel operations.

Future releases (v2.0)

Online payment integration and third-party service connectors (OTAs, channel managers) are planned for subsequent phases — not in initial release.

5. Significance & Literature

Impact & Benefits

  • Reduced booking errors & double-reservations
  • Faster customer service & real-time updates
  • Centralized accurate record-keeping
  • Scalable for business growth

Literature Insight

Research shows automated hotel systems improve resource utilization and minimize human error. Modern web-based reservation platforms are no longer a luxury but a competitive necessity in the hospitality industry. Digital transformation drives guest satisfaction and revenue optimization.
